Biography

A Scientific and Humanitarian Journey

Prof. Dr. Ahmed Elwakeel is an Associate Professor in the Department of Cardiothoracic Surgery at the Faculty of Medicine, Cairo University — one of the region's leading specialists in complex open-heart surgery for adults and children across the Middle East and Africa. He graduated from Cairo University Faculty of Medicine and completed his surgical training at specialist centres both in Egypt and internationally.

"In medicine, we move toward the most dangerous possibility first to rule it out — not toward the easiest. That protects patients from decisions that seem logical but are not the right ones."

His expertise spans valve surgery — including mitral valve repair and replacement, aortic valve surgery, and the Ozaki procedure — as well as coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) both on-pump and Off-Pump, ascending aortic surgery, and redo operations that other centres decline. He is known for taking on cases referred as inoperable and providing an independent, evidence-based assessment of surgical risk.

In paediatric cardiac surgery, he performs operations for congenital defects in infants and children and follows patients with congenital heart disease into adulthood (GUCH — Grown-Up Congenital Heart Disease). He believes that the correct timing of intervention makes a fundamental difference to long-term quality of life.

He founded Egypt Heart Clinic with a clear philosophy: every patient receives an accurate diagnosis, a written treatment plan in a language they understand, and the full truth — including cases where declining surgery is the smarter decision. International patients receive second opinions in English or Arabic with a written report within 48 hours.

Our Philosophy

Three Principles We Never Compromise

These are not slogans — they are the daily decisions that govern how we work.

Honesty Before Surgery

We only recommend surgery when it is genuinely the right choice. If watchful waiting, medication, or simply doing nothing is the smarter option, we say so clearly — even if that means we don't operate.

A Written Plan You Can Understand

Every patient leaves with a written treatment plan in their language — explaining diagnosis, options, risks, and expected outcomes. No unexplained medical jargon. No decisions without understanding. English or Arabic.

The Patient Is a Partner in the Decision

We believe a patient is not simply a patient — they are a person with the right to know and to choose. Our role is to explain clearly, support fully, and present options honestly. The final decision is always theirs.

Surgical Specialties

What We Treat

Six surgical specialties led by a team sharing the same philosophy and the same standards.

Adult Cardiac Surgery

Valve repair and replacement, coronary artery bypass (CABG), ascending aorta, and redo operations.

Paediatric & Congenital

Congenital heart defects in infants and children, and follow-up into adulthood (GUCH programme).

Thoracic Surgery

Lung and mediastinal tumours, VATS (minimally invasive), pectus excavatum, and pleural disease.

Redo Operations

Cases previously operated at other centres requiring re-intervention — precise risk assessment and a clear plan.

Second Medical Opinion

Independent review of your scans and reports within 48 hours — written report, video explanation, English or Arabic.

High-Risk & Complex Cases

Diabetes, renal impairment, poor ventricular function, elderly patients — objective assessment, not blanket refusal.
