Independent expert review

Second Opinion Service

Before any surgical decision, get an honest, independent assessment of your case from Prof. Dr. Ahmed Elwakeel. No travel required. Written report returned within 48 hours.

  1. 1

    Send your files via WhatsApp

    Share your existing reports, imaging, and a brief medical history to +201009831083. Photos of printed reports are accepted — no special format required.

  2. 2

    The team reviews everything

    Prof. Elwakeel personally reviews the case and prepares a written assessment covering diagnosis, options, risks, and recommendation — including if the right answer is not to operate.

  3. 3

    Receive your written report

    You receive the written report within 48 hours, in English or Arabic. A video consultation to walk through the findings is available if needed — from wherever you are.

  4. 4

    Decide with full clarity

    The report is yours to keep and share with other doctors. There is no pressure, no obligation, and no follow-up unless you choose to reach out.

What to send

  • Catheterisation / angiography reports and images
  • Echocardiography (echo) reports
  • CT or MRI scans (DICOM files or high-res photos)
  • Most recent blood tests and ECG
  • Previous surgical or procedure reports (if any)
  • A brief description of current symptoms

Patients from outside Egypt

International Patients

Whether you are in the Gulf, Europe, North America, or anywhere else — getting expert cardiac care from Cairo is more accessible than you may think.

English and Arabic — your choice

All communication, written reports, and treatment plans are available in both English and Arabic. No interpreter required. The team responds in whichever language you prefer.

Start entirely remotely

Send your existing reports via WhatsApp. The second opinion, diagnosis review, and initial treatment plan are prepared without you needing to travel. Travel is only required if you decide to proceed with surgery.

48-hour response

Second opinion reports are returned within 48 hours of receiving all required files. Urgent cases are prioritised — contact the team via WhatsApp to flag urgency.

Surgery in accredited hospitals

If surgery is recommended and you choose to proceed, operations take place at Salam International Hospital and Dar Al Fouad Hospital in Cairo — both internationally recognised private hospitals with modern cardiac facilities.

Coordination for your visit

The clinic team can assist with scheduling, timing of tests before arrival, and guidance on logistics. Patients travelling for surgery typically need 10–21 days in Egypt depending on the procedure.

Written treatment plan to share

Every patient receives a written treatment plan they can share with their local doctors. If surgery elsewhere is a better option for any reason, we will say so directly.
